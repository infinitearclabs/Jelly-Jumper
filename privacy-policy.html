Jelly Jumper Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 7, 2026

Infinite Arc Labs ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile game Jelly Jumper (the "Game").

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use Jelly Jumper. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about the third-party services used by the Game.

1. Information We Collect

Jelly Jumper does not require you to create an account or provide us directly with personal information such as your name, email address, telephone number, or physical address.

The Game may use third-party services that process certain information automatically when the Game is used. This may include information associated with advertising, device operation, purchases, and application usage, depending on the services and settings applicable to your device and region.

2. Advertising

Jelly Jumper uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information in connection with the delivery, measurement, and personalization of advertisements. The information processed by these services may include device-related information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, and information about interactions with advertisements, subject to applicable laws and your privacy choices.

We do not directly receive or control all information that may be collected or processed by advertising providers. Google's processing of information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

Jelly Jumper uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent and privacy choices where required by applicable law.

Depending on your location and applicable privacy requirements, you may be shown a consent or privacy options form before advertisements are provided.

Your available choices may affect whether personalized advertising or other forms of advertising are used.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices through Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. In-App Purchases

Jelly Jumper offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.

Payments and purchase processing are handled by Google Play. We do not directly collect or store your payment card number, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

Google Play may process information necessary to complete and manage purchases. This processing is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

The Game may also check for previously completed purchases so that eligible purchases can be restored.

5. Local Game Data

Jelly Jumper may store game-related information locally on your device, such as game progress, settings, and in-game currency or other gameplay-related information.

This information is used to provide game functionality and is not intended to identify you personally.

Deleting the Game or clearing its application data may remove locally stored game information.

6. Children's Privacy

Jelly Jumper is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children. Because the Game uses third-party advertising and other services, applicable age, consent, and privacy requirements may affect the services available to users in different regions.

7. Third-Party Services

Jelly Jumper uses third-party services to provide advertising and in-app purchasing functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

The principal third-party services currently used by the Game include:

  • Google AdMob — advertising services
  • Google User Messaging Platform — consent management
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchase processing

We recommend reviewing Google's privacy documentation for additional information about how Google handles information:

Google Privacy Policy

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

Jelly Jumper itself does not maintain a user account or a personal profile for players.

Information processed by third-party providers such as Google may be retained according to their respective policies and legal requirements.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Game, the services we use, or applicable legal requirements.

When this policy is updated, the effective date at the top of this page will be changed accordingly.

This Privacy Policy is intended to provide general information about the privacy practices associated with Jelly Jumper. Additional privacy rights may apply to users depending on their country, state, or region.
